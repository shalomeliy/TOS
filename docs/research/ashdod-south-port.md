# Research - Ashdod South Port (Hadarom Container Terminal, HCT)

Date: 2026-10-22. Public sources only. Confidence: **[confirmed]** = official or two independent sources agree; **[single source]**; **[conflict]**; **[unknown]**; **[assumption]** = my inference, not a fact.

## Operator
- HCT (Hadarom Container Terminal Ltd), owned by TIL (Terminal Investment Limited, MSC group). **[confirmed]** hadct.co.il, he.wikipedia
- Partial operation Aug 2021, first vessel Feb 2022. **[single source]** he.wikipedia
- Described as semi-automated: automated yard, remote operation from a control room. **[confirmed]** hadct.co.il, unisco
- **TOS: Konecranes** supplied the cranes plus a Terminal Operating System and crane control system (order reported Q1 2019). **[single source]** inforMARE / worldcargonews snippets. So HCT is very likely **not** running Navis N4. The simulator stays "N4-style" as a learning model.

## Area and quays
- Total area 1,300 dunam; container terminal 640 dunam (~64 ha). CGR: 63 ha of stacking yards. **[confirmed]**
- Quay 27: 800 m, depth 17.3 m, containers and general cargo. **[confirmed]**
- Quay 28: 445 m (port listing) or 500 m (CGR, wiki). **[conflict]**. General cargo approved Apr 2022.
- Designed for ships up to ~18,000 TEU. **[single source]** Dutch Dredging
- Planned capacity 1.1 million TEU/year. **[single source]** (another site says 1.7M; not trusted)

## Equipment
- Yard: **Automatic Stacking Cranes (ASC), Konecranes, 30 units**, delivered in 5 shipments, last in Nov 2021. **[single source]** heavyliftnews (BigLift)
- Ship-to-shore (STS) cranes: TIL planning info said **8 STS** on one quay. **[single source, not confirmed delivered]**
- The 5 ZPMC STS cranes in the news are for the old Ashdod Port (Pier 21), **not** HCT. **[confirmed]**
- Horizontal transport between quay and yard (trucks / tractors / carriers): **[unknown]**
- ASC layout: 30 ASCs usually means about 15 blocks with 2 cranes each, blocks running perpendicular to the quay. **[assumption]**

## Rail
- Rail cargo terminal opened Sep 2024, linked by a 2.2 km underground branch. **[single source]** he.wikipedia

## Working time
- Gate hours (hadct.co.il): Sunday-Thursday 05:00-24:00; Friday and holiday eves 05:00-14:45; Saturday closed. **[confirmed - official]**
- Operations run "24/6" (no Shabbat). **[confirmed]** hadct.co.il, job ads
- **User rule for the simulator:** Friday work stops 15:00, resumes Saturday night (Motzei Shabbat) 20:00.
